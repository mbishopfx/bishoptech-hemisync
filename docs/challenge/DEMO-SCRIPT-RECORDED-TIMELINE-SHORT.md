# Cognistration — compact voiceover pickups

Use these alternate lines if the primary voiceover feels too slow, if a UI
wait gets shortened further, or if you need a faster reshoot. They preserve the
same story while leaving more room for natural pauses.

## Home and handoff

> Cognistration starts with an intention, then turns it into a session. I ask
> the agent for a bedtime direction, and it opens the generator right inside
> the conversation while playback stays under my control.

## Machine and guide

> Now the machine and the guide work together. I can hear the preview, adjust
> the session, and move through the science explanation without losing the
> current state. It is an interactive tool with clear boundaries, not a
> medical claim.

## Science and signup

> I can ask for a deeper science explanation, then ask for an account. The
> guide stays educational, and the signup form stays user-controlled: my
> credentials never become MCP arguments.

## Purchase and mobile

> When I ask about buying a tone, the agent explains the option first, then
> opens a reviewable purchase surface with delivery details, a copy prompt,
> and the real iPhone App Store path.

## MCP catalog outro

> The full experience is exposed through MCP: discovery, tone search,
> recommendations, calibration, session plans, recipes, policy, signup,
> downloads, payments, delivery, and workshop access. The machine is live too,
> with exact controls, relative speed changes, direction presets, preview,
> fullscreen, the science guide, and feedback. The skills keep the whole flow
> grounded.
